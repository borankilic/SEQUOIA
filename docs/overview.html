Validated against

  • Malus, HWP rotation, thin lens, telescope, Gaussian overlap
  • MZI visibility = |γ(τ)|; CHSH S = 2√2·V; unheralded g2 = 1+1/K
  • HOM curve vs spdcalc; dead time vs Monte Carlo
  • 777 tests; three independent review rounds on the quantum layer

Known limits

  • Absolute SPDC rate uncertain (×0.4 to ×2): use a measured brightness
  • Collection modes are part data, not back-propagated
  • Thin lenses, mode at centre wavelength, no GDD/PMD
  • Feed-forward only: no cavities, no back-reflections
